Go to *Settings > Companies > Configure Document Layout* and choose the
**DIN 5008** layout. The tab **Footer** of the layout settings offers the tax
number lines of `l10n_de_report_footer` and two settings of this layout:

- **Show Bank Account Holder**: print the account holder below the name of
  the bank for every bank account of the footer.
- **Column Widths (%)**: widths of the four columns of the footer table
  (company address, footer text, tax numbers, bank accounts) in percent,
  separated by commas, for example `25,35,20,20`. The values must add up to
  100; empty keeps the widths of the layout.
