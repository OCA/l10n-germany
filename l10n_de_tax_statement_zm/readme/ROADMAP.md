- Check the journal items behind the ZM, e.g. a tax which doesn't match
  the fiscal position of the partner. The approval only checks the VAT
  numbers and the countries of the ZM lines.
- Triangular transactions (Dreiecksgeschäfte, Kennzahl 42, type D) aren't
  part of the ZM, only the deliveries (L, Kennzahl 41) and the services
  (S, Kennzahl 21).
- The ZM always has the period of the statement: a company which
  declares the statement quarterly but has to declare the ZM monthly
  (intra-community deliveries above 50,000 euros per quarter) can't
  create the monthly ZM.
