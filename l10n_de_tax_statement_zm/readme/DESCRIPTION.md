This module extends the *German VAT Statement*
(Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung) with the *Zusammenfassende Meldung* (ZM,
EC Sales List) of the same period.

The ZM is based on Kennzahl 41 (innergemeinschaftliche Lieferungen an
Abnehmer mit USt-IdNr.) and Kennzahl 21 (nicht steuerbare sonstige
Leistungen) of the statement. For every customer it shows:

- the VAT identification number and the country
- the amounts of the deliveries of products and of the services, in two
  columns

The approval of the statement attaches the ZM as CSV file for the
upload in Mein ELSTER, with separate lines for products and services.
