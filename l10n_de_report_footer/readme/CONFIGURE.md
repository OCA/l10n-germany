Go to *Settings > Companies > Configure Document Layout*.

- **Address**, **Tagline** and **Footer**: write the texts. Type `/` or use
  the placeholder button of the editor to insert company data, for example
  the phone number, the VAT number or a bank account. Placeholders on the
  usual company fields (address, phone, e-mail, website, VAT, company
  registry, ...) can be used by everyone; other expressions are reserved to
  administrators and members of the *Mail Template Editor* group, like in
  mail templates.

The layout settings below the paper format are grouped in tabs. Every change
is shown in the preview at once; the settings are stored with the company
when the wizard is saved.

Tab **Footer** (with the module `l10n_de`):

- **Show St.-Nr.** and **Show W-IdNr.**: print the tax number and the
  business identification number of the company as labelled lines of the
  company block of every layout.

Tab **Page**:

- **Bottom Margin (mm)**: bottom page margin reserved for the footer, between
  5 and 60 mm. 0 keeps the margin of the paper format. Clear the field to
  restore the default.

While one of the texts or the bottom margin field is focused, the matching
area of the preview (address block, tagline, footer text or footer) is
outlined.
